
# My Favorite Movie: Bird Box

## About the Movie

**Bird Box** is a 2018 post-apocalyptic horror and thriller movie starring **Sandra Bullock**.

The story follows a mother named Malorie who tries to survive with two children in a dangerous world where mysterious creatures cause people to harm themselves when they see them.

## Why I Like This Movie

I like *Bird Box* because it is:
- Suspenseful and exciting.
- Full of unexpected moments.
- Interesting and mysterious.
- A movie that shows courage and survival.
